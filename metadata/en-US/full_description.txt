SimpleXray is an Xray-core client for Android 10 and later.

• Import servers and subscriptions or enter connection settings manually.
• VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks/SS2022, SOCKS, HTTP, WireGuard and Hysteria 2.
• Domain, IP, GeoSite and GeoIP rules with a separate server for each routing block.
• DNS, FakeIP and SplitDNS.
• Import and export routing presets through the clipboard.
• Per-app VPN selection and local SOCKS/HTTP proxies.
• Profile JSON, configuration overrides and core logs.
• Connection speed, traffic totals and disconnect action in the notification.
• Optional automatic connection after reboot.

Source and releases: https://github.com/IvanTopGaming/SimpleXray
